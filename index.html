Human first, tinkerer second, software architect by trade. I've been writing software since 2007 and taking things apart for longer than that.

As a human

I live in Rawalpindi. I drink more tea than is probably sensible, go for long walks when I need to think, and follow the news more closely than is good for me. Most of the rest of my time goes to family.

I'm curious by default. If something interests me, I want to know how it works, and I'm happiest when I'm learning something new with my hands.

As a tinkerer

I trained as an electrical engineer, which is a polite way of saying I've never fully trusted a box I couldn't open. Over the years that instinct has turned into:

  • A homelab A personal server I keep rebuilding, breaking, and fixing. Mostly to learn, partly because it's fun.
  • LLM experiments Working out where large language models genuinely help with everyday learning and productivity, and where they just get in the way.
  • A trail of side quests Embedded C on a real-time OS, mobile games in Unity, network traffic analysis tools in C and C++, chat servers built on Socket.IO and Redis.

Most of it never ships anywhere. That's fine. The point is the understanding.
